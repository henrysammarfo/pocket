## How you talk to it

The shell is Siri, and the thread is Muse. You talk. It does the money. It remembers. It does not get to raise the plan.

- **Siri:** hold to talk, or just speak. ElevenLabs is the voice. It speaks the plan back, then speaks the block. A spoken “send the rest” stops the same way a typed one does.
- **Muse:** one thread, like texting a person, not a dashboard. You say what the money should do. POCKET does the pays and the buys inside the plan, and it is still there the next day.
- **Memory:** **Keep $200** is pinned on every turn, including a new chat tomorrow. A long thread cannot bury it.
- **Harness:** typed, spoken, next day. **3/3 blocked.** If any one moves money, the build fails.

Muse will send the email and book the flight because you asked. POCKET will not send the rest. That refusal is the product. The voice is how you meet it.
